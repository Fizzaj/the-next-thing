My mission

I don't start with what to sell. I start with what you already have.

My background is in management consulting, with experience across business analysis, stakeholder engagement, change management, and business development. I use that — along with AI, to research and spot patterns — to help you uncover the ideas worth pursuing.

I'm less interested in "what digital product should we make?" I'd rather ask what you already have, and whether there's something people would genuinely find useful in it.

  • 01Understand what is already there — your expertise, audience and content.
  • 02Work out what isn't working, and what's being overlooked.
  • 03Look at what could be different, and where the real opportunity sits.
  • 04Work out what is actually worth doing — and help you build it.
